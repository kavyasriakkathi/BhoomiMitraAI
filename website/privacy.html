Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BhoomiMitra AI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an early-stage agricultural AI initiative developing a WhatsApp-first assistant to help farmers access agricultural information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you interact with our public website and WhatsApp assistant, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

In our current pilot phase, we collect information necessary to provide and refine agricultural assistance:

  • Communications on WhatsApp: Text inquiries, voice notes, and crop photos sent to our WhatsApp assistant number.
  • Agricultural Context: Information you voluntarily share about your farm (e.g., crop types, acreage, location/village, observation notes).
  • Contact Information: Phone number (via WhatsApp) and email address if you contact us for partnerships or inquiries.
  • Technical Data: Basic server logs when visiting our website (browser type, timestamp).

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Processing and answering your agricultural questions using language and vision AI models.
  • Providing relevant crop advisory and locating verified nearby agricultural shops where stock information is available.
  • Improving model accuracy, speech recognition for regional dialects, and agricultural retrieval relevance in pilot areas.
  • Responding directly to partner, expert, or farmer inquiries.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. To operate the WhatsApp-first pilot, data is processed using reputable service providers:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business API: To send and receive messages according to Meta’s Business Messaging terms.
  • Cloud & AI Infrastructure: Secure cloud providers hosting speech-to-text, vision, and language inference models.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain messages only as long as necessary to maintain contextual continuity for your farming queries and evaluate pilot model safety. You may request deletion of your conversation history and farmer profile at any time by contacting us at contact@bhoomimitra.ai.

5. Scope & Pilot Disclaimer

BhoomiMitra AI is an early-stage initiative currently conducting controlled real-world validation in Korutla, Telangana. We do not claim formal government certifications or international compliance seals beyond our commitment to responsible, privacy-respecting early testing.
