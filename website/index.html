How BhoomiMitra Works

A frictionless 4-step process designed to bring actionable agricultural support to any farmer in seconds.

01

Ask

Send a simple voice note or text message in your mother tongue detailing what you observe in your field.

02

Share

Send a clear photo of the affected leaf, pest, or crop when visual inspection is needed.

03

Understand

BhoomiMitra processes the farmer’s request using agricultural AI, cross-referencing knowledge and crop context.

04

Act

Receive a clear response and useful next steps, including verified input availability where supported.

💡 BhoomiMitra is designed to communicate in a farmer-friendly way rather than requiring technical knowledge.

Responsible Agricultural AI

Safety, Responsibility & Trust

Agriculture is high-stakes. Decisions impact a farmer’s livelihoods, food safety, and soil health. BhoomiMitra AI operates under strict responsible AI principles:

✓

Verification Before Real-World Action

All agricultural guidance provided by BhoomiMitra should be verified with local agronomy officers or trusted village experts before large-scale application.

✓

Careful Chemical & Pesticide Handling

We do not dispense indiscriminate chemical dosages. Recommendations emphasize integrated pest management (IPM), safety precautions, and expert cross-checking.

✓

Assistant, Not Replacement for Experts

BhoomiMitra AI is an assistive digital companion, never a legal replacement for qualified agricultural extension officers or certified agronomists.

✓

Low Confidence & Ambiguity Protocols

When image clarity is ambiguous or symptom patterns overlap, BhoomiMitra proactively declines over-confident assertions and directs the farmer to local human experts.

✓

Only Verified Shop & Stock Data

Shop locations and product inventory availability are surfaced exclusively when verified by participating dealers in our pilot directory.

✓

No Unsupported Claims

We do not claim official government regulatory endorsements or 100% predictive accuracy. We state what the technology can and cannot do honestly.

About the Project

About BhoomiMitra AI

BhoomiMitra AI is an early-stage agricultural AI initiative focused on making farming information more accessible through WhatsApp, voice, images and multilingual interaction.

Founded on the belief that technological innovation should adapt to the farmer rather than asking the farmer to adapt to complicated software, we combine modern language and vision models with grounded agricultural knowledge bases.

We prioritize scientific discipline, clear user safety warnings, and tangible connections with verified local suppliers over hype.
