BhoomiMitra AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

BhoomiMitra AI ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered WhatsApp agricultural assistant that helps farmers with crop advice, weather guidance, and agri-scheme information. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with BhoomiMitra AI through WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and display name
  • Text messages you send to the assistant
  • Voice messages and audio transcriptions (if sent)
  • Crop images you share for disease diagnosis
  • Farmer profile details you voluntarily provide (district, state, crop type, land size)
  • Conversation history and AI response logs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalised crop advice and agronomic recommendations
  • To remember your farming profile across conversations
  • To improve the quality and accuracy of AI responses
  • To identify and suggest relevant government agri-schemes
  • To maintain service security and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share anonymised, aggregate data for agricultural research. Your data is processed using third-party AI services (Google Gemini) solely to generate farming responses. These services are bound by their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

Your personal data is retained for as long as you use the service. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 6). Some anonymised logs may be retained briefly for security and abuse prevention purposes.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted database storage and HTTPS-only communication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the service

To request data deletion, visit our Data Deletion Instructions page or send an email to kavyasriakkathi@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

BhoomiMitra AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
