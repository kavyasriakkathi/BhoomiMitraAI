Privacy Policy & Platform Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

At BhoomiMitra AI, we take your data privacy and rights seriously. In accordance with Meta Platform Policy and global data protection regulations, you have the full right to request the deletion of your account and personal data collected through our WhatsApp Assistant.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your personal data stored in the BhoomiMitra AI platform through the following method:

Option 1: Direct Email Deletion Request (Recommended)

Send an email to our official privacy contact with your WhatsApp phone number (including country code) and the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

✉ Request Data Deletion (kavyasriakkathi@gmail.com)

Option 2: WhatsApp Chat Command

Send the text message "DELETE MY DATA" directly to our WhatsApp Assistant (+91 77948 52530). Our system will automatically initiate the deletion process for your registered phone number.

Personal Data Subject to Deletion

Upon receiving your deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our databases:

  • Your phone number and WhatsApp profile name
  • Farmer profile metadata (district, state, land size, current crops)
  • Full conversation history, user messages, and AI responses
  • Voice recordings, audio transcriptions, and uploaded crop images
  • Saved crop health diagnosis history and agronomic recommendations
  • Farmer long-term memory profiles and personalization settings

Data Retention Exceptions

Some limited information may be retained temporarily under specific circumstances:

  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Transaction or log data required to be kept by applicable law or regulatory obligations.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Anonymized security logs retained briefly to prevent fraud or abuse of the service.
  • Anonymized Agronomic Data: De-identified, aggregate statistical data (with all personal identifiers permanently stripped) used to improve crop disease models.

Processing Timeline

Once a deletion request is verified, your personal data will be completely deleted from our primary servers and database within 30 days. A final confirmation email or WhatsApp notification will be sent upon completion.

🔒 Security Note: BhoomiMitra AI will never ask for your passwords, bank account PINs, or sensitive financial information. Please do not include password details in your deletion request email.
