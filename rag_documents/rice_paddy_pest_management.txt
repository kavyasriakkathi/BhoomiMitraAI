RICE AND PADDY PEST MANAGEMENT GUIDE
Source: ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)
State: Telangana | Crop: Rice / Paddy
Language: English (Telugu translation available via BhoomiMitra)

=============================================================
1. MAJOR PESTS OF RICE/PADDY IN TELANGANA
=============================================================

1.1 STEM BORER (కాండం తొలుచు పురుగు)
- Symptoms: Dead hearts in vegetative stage, white ears at heading stage
- Control:
  * Remove and destroy egg masses from seedlings before transplanting
  * Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 18 kg/acre at 15 days after transplanting
  * Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml per acre
  * Spray Monocrotophos 36 SL @ 400 ml/acre mixed in 200 litres water
- Biological control: Release Trichogramma japonicum @ 1 lakh/acre at egg laying stage

1.2 LEAF FOLDER (ఆకు చుట్టు పురుగు)
- Symptoms: Leaves rolled longitudinally, whitish papery streaks
- Control:
  * Spray Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 400 ml/acre
  * Spray Quinalphos 25 EC @ 400 ml/acre
  * Spray Indoxacarb 14.5 SC @ 160 ml/acre
  * Avoid excess nitrogen application

1.3 BROWN PLANT HOPPER (BPH) (గోధుమ రంగు రసం పీల్చే పురుగు)
- Most dangerous pest - causes "hopper burn"
- Symptoms: Plants dry out in circular patches (hopper burn)
- Control:
  * Spray Buprofezin 25 SC @ 400 ml/acre
  * Spray Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 40 g/acre
  * Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG @ 120 g/acre
  * Do NOT spray synthetic pyrethroids - they increase BPH population
  * Keep field bunds clean, avoid water stagnation

1.4 GALL MIDGE (పిత్త దోమ)
- Symptoms: Central shoot turns into a tubular gall (silver shoot / onion shoot)
- Control:
  * Use resistant varieties: MTU 1010, MTU 7029, Rajendra Mahsuri
  * Apply Carbofuran 3G @ 10 kg/acre
  * Spray Monocrotophos 36 SL @ 400 ml/acre

1.5 THRIPS (తీగె పురుగు)
- Symptoms: Leaf tips rolled, silvery streaks on leaves
- Control:
  * Spray Acephate 75 SP @ 300 g/acre
  * Spray Fipronil 5 SC @ 400 ml/acre
  * Flood irrigate the field if possible

=============================================================
2. MAJOR DISEASES OF RICE/PADDY
=============================================================

2.1 BLAST DISEASE (బ్లాస్ట్ వ్యాధి)
- Most serious disease in Telangana
- Symptoms: Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves with grey centre and brown border
- Control:
  * Seed treatment with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2g/kg seed
  * Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 120 g/acre at first sign
  * Spray Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 300 ml/acre
  * Use resistant varieties: MTU 1010, BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri)

2.2 BACTERIAL LEAF BLIGHT (BLB) (బ్యాక్టీరియా పత్ర వాడకం)
- Symptoms: Water-soaked yellowing from leaf tips and margins
- Control:
  * No chemical control is fully effective
  * Use resistant varieties
  * Spray Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 500 g/acre
  * Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer
  * Drain water from field

2.3 SHEATH BLIGHT (తొడిమ కుళ్ళు)
- Symptoms: Oval lesions on leaf sheath with grey center and brown border
- Control:
  * Spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 200 ml/acre
  * Spray Hexaconazole 5 SC @ 300 ml/acre
  * Spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 400 g/acre
  * Avoid dense planting, ensure proper spacing

=============================================================
3. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) FOR PADDY
=============================================================

Preventive Measures:
- Use certified, disease-free seeds
- Seed treatment before sowing
- Balanced fertilizer application (do not over-apply nitrogen)
- Proper spacing: 20x15 cm for transplanting
- Field sanitation: remove crop debris after harvest
- Install pheromone traps @ 5/acre for stem borer monitoring
- Light traps @ 1/acre to monitor pest populations
- Maintain water level at 5 cm to discourage BPH

Spray Calendar for Paddy:
- 15 DAT: Stem borer spray (if needed)
- 30 DAT: Check for BPH and leaf folder
- 45 DAT: Blast spray (preventive during monsoon)
- 60 DAT: Sheath blight spray (if needed)
- 75 DAT: Final check before harvest
