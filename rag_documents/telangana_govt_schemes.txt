GOVERNMENT SCHEMES FOR FARMERS - TELANGANA
Source: Government of India & Telangana State Government
State: Telangana | Category: Government Schemes
Language: English

=============================================================
1. RYTHU BANDHU SCHEME (రైతు బంధు)
=============================================================

What is it?
- Investment support scheme by Telangana Government
- Gives ₹10,000 per acre per year to farmers
- Paid in 2 installments: ₹5,000 before Kharif + ₹5,000 before Rabi season

Who is eligible?
- All farmers who own agricultural land in Telangana
- Both patta and assigned land holders are eligible
- No income limit - all farmers qualify

How to apply / receive:
- No application needed - automatic if land records are updated
- Amount directly credited to farmer's bank account linked to Aadhar
- Check status: rythu.telangana.gov.in or contact Village Revenue Officer (VRO)

Documents needed to register:
- Patta / land document
- Aadhar card
- Bank account (linked to Aadhar)
- Mobile number

Helpline: 1800-180-2030 (Toll Free)

=============================================================
2. PM-KISAN YOJANA (PM కిసాన్)
=============================================================

What is it?
- Central Government income support scheme
- ₹6,000 per year to all small and marginal farmers
- Paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each

Who is eligible?
- Farmers with agricultural land up to 2 hectares (5 acres)
- Must have Aadhar-linked bank account
- Land records must be updated

How to apply:
- Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC / MeeSeva)
- Apply online at pmkisan.gov.in
- Register through Agriculture Department

Documents needed:
- Aadhar card
- Bank passbook
- Land documents (Patta/7-12 extract)
- Mobile number

Check PM-Kisan status: pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status"
PM-Kisan Helpline: 011-24300606 | 155261

3. PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY)
   (పంట బీమా పథకం)
=============================================================

What is it?
- Crop insurance scheme for farmers
- Covers losses due to natural calamities, pest/disease, drought, flood

Premium rates (Telangana):
- Kharif crops: 2% of Sum Insured
- Rabi crops: 1.5% of Sum Insured
- Commercial/Horticulture: 5% of Sum Insured

How to enroll:
- Through bank (if you have crop loan, enrollment is automatic)
- Visit nearest bank branch or CSC center
- Enroll before cut-off date (usually 1 week before sowing)

Claim process:
- Notify within 72 hours of crop damage: Call 14447 or inform local Agriculture Officer
- Claim assessed by government team
- Amount credited directly to bank account

Helpline: 14447 (Crop Insurance Helpline)

=============================================================
4. RYTHU VEDIKA (రైతు వేదిక)
=============================================================

What is it?
- Telangana scheme to provide free advisory services to farmers
- Regular meetings at village level with Agriculture Officers
- Free soil testing, seed distribution guidance

How to access:
- Contact your local Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO)
- Visit nearest Rythu Vedika center
- Call: 1800-425-1110

=============================================================
5. KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) (కిసాన్ క్రెడిట్ కార్డ్)
=============================================================

What is it?
- Easy credit for farmers at low interest rate (4% per year after subvention)
- Can use for seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, irrigation

Loan amount:
- Based on land holding and crop
- Usually ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000

How to apply:
- Visit nearest bank branch (SBI, Andhra Bank, District Cooperative Bank)
- Bring: Aadhar, land documents, passport photo, bank account

=============================================================
6. MICRO IRRIGATION (నీటి పొదుపు పథకం)
=============================================================

Subsidies available:
- Drip Irrigation: 55% to 90% subsidy (depends on farmer category)
- Sprinkler Irrigation: 55% to 90% subsidy
- SC/ST farmers get higher subsidy

Apply through:
- Online: pmksy.gov.in
- District Agriculture Office

=============================================================
7. SOIL HEALTH CARD (మట్టి ఆరోగ్య కార్డ్)
=============================================================

- Free soil testing by government
- Personalized fertilizer recommendation based on your soil
- Visit nearest Soil Testing Laboratory
- One card valid for 3 years
