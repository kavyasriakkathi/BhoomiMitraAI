PADDY FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATION GUIDE
Source: Telangana State Agriculture Department & ICAR
State: Telangana | Crop: Rice / Paddy
Language: English

=============================================================
1. RECOMMENDED FERTILIZER DOSES FOR PADDY (PER ACRE)
=============================================================

General Recommendation (Telangana):
- Nitrogen (N): 80 kg/acre (as Urea)
- Phosphorus (P): 40 kg/acre (as DAP or SSP)
- Potassium (K): 40 kg/acre (as MOP)

Urea Requirement:
- Total Urea needed = 175 kg/acre
- Split into 3 doses:
  * Dose 1 (Basal - at transplanting): 58 kg Urea/acre
  * Dose 2 (Tillering - 21 days after transplanting): 58 kg Urea/acre
  * Dose 3 (Panicle initiation - 45 days after transplanting): 58 kg Urea/acre

DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate):
- Apply 87 kg DAP/acre as basal dose before transplanting
- Mix into soil before puddling

MOP (Muriate of Potash):
- Apply 67 kg MOP/acre
- Split: 50% basal + 50% at panicle initiation

=============================================================
2. ZINC DEFICIENCY (COMMON IN TELANGANA)
=============================================================

Symptoms of Zinc Deficiency:
- Brown rusty spots on leaves
- Stunted plant growth
- Leaves turn pale yellow to white (khaira disease)
- Most common in alkaline soils and flooded conditions

Treatment:
- Apply Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) @ 10 kg/acre as basal dose
- If deficiency appears after transplanting: spray 0.5% ZnSO4 solution
  (dissolve 500g ZnSO4 in 100 litres water, spray per acre)
- Apply ZnSO4 once every 3 seasons

=============================================================
3. BORON DEFICIENCY
=============================================================

Symptoms:
- White tips on young leaves
- Empty grains (poor seed setting)

Treatment:
- Spray Borax 0.2% solution at panicle initiation stage
- (Dissolve 200g Borax in 100 litres water)

=============================================================
4. BASAL FERTILIZER SCHEDULE (BEFORE TRANSPLANTING)
=============================================================

Step 1 - Soil preparation:
- Apply 2 tonnes FYM (Farm Yard Manure) or compost per acre
- Mix well into soil before last ploughing

Step 2 - Basal chemical fertilizers (apply 1 day before transplanting):
- DAP: 87 kg/acre
- MOP: 33 kg/acre
- Zinc Sulphate: 10 kg/acre (if not applied in last 3 seasons)

=============================================================
5. TOP DRESSING SCHEDULE
=============================================================

21 Days After Transplanting (Tillering stage):
- Urea: 58 kg/acre
- Apply when field has 2-3 cm water
- Broadcast evenly, do not let water drain for 3 days

45 Days After Transplanting (Panicle initiation):
- Urea: 58 kg/acre
- MOP: 33 kg/acre
- This is the most critical dose for good grain filling

=============================================================
6. ORGANIC ALTERNATIVES (FOR ORGANIC FARMERS)
=============================================================

Green Manure:
- Grow Dhaincha (Sesbania) and incorporate 30 days before transplanting
- Provides 30-40 kg N/acre equivalent

Bio-fertilizers:
- Azospirillum: 2 packets (200g each)/acre - apply to nursery or roots before transplanting
- Blue-Green Algae (BGA): 2 kg/acre - apply to flooded field
- PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria): 2 packets/acre

Jeevamrutham:
- Mix 10 litres cow urine + 10 kg cow dung + 2 kg jaggery + 2 kg gram flour in 200 litres water
- Ferment for 7 days, dilute 1:10, apply to field every 21 days

=============================================================
7. COMMON FERTILIZER MISTAKES TO AVOID
=============================================================

- Do NOT apply urea on dry soil (evaporation waste)
- Do NOT apply urea when crop is water stressed
- Do NOT apply excess nitrogen (increases pest and disease problems)
- Do NOT mix DAP and Urea together (causes nitrogen loss)
- Always apply fertilizer in the morning (before 9 AM) or evening (after 4 PM)

=============================================================
8. SOIL TESTING RECOMMENDATION
=============================================================

- Test soil every 3 years at nearest Soil Testing Lab
- Telangana Soil Testing Labs: Available in every district headquarters
- For custom fertilizer recommendation based on soil test: Contact local Agriculture Officer or KVK
