Title: Cotton Alternaria Leaf Spot Disease Management Guide
Crop: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / Gossypium spp.)
Disease: Alternaria Leaf Spot
Causal Agents: Alternaria macrospora / Alternaria alternata
Disease Type: Fungal disease
Target Region: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and All India Cotton Zones

1. Symptoms:
- Small dark-brown to reddish-brown circular or irregular necrotic spots on cotton leaves.
- Lesions may show concentric rings or a target-board appearance.
- Spots may have a yellowish chlorotic halo surrounding the necrotic center.
- Severe infection can cause spots to merge, leading to leaf blighting, premature yellowing, and defoliation.

2. Identification Guidance:
- Inspect lower and middle canopy leaves where the disease first manifests under humid, cloudy, or rainy weather conditions.
- Look for target-like concentric rings on leaf spots with yellow halos.

3. Cultural and Agronomic Management:
- Remove and properly dispose of infected plant debris and fallen leaves after harvest to reduce fungal inoculum.
- Maintain balanced NPK nutrition across all crop growth stages.
- Avoid potassium deficiency because nutritional imbalance can increase disease severity and susceptibility to Alternaria infection.

4. Fungicide Management:
- Fungicide application should begin at the first signs of leaf spot where recommended by an agricultural authority or registered product label.
- Repeat application intervals must follow verified agricultural recommendations and registered product labels.
- Do not recommend unnecessary repeated spraying.

5. Verified Dosage Information Provided for this Knowledge Entry:
- Mancozeb 75% WP:
  Dosage: 2.5 to 3.0 g per litre of water.
  Approximate field quantity: 500 to 600 g per acre in 200 L water.
- Copper Oxychloride 50% WP:
  Dosage: 3.0 g per litre of water.
  Approximate field quantity: 600 g per acre in 200 L water.
- Propiconazole 25% EC:
  Dosage: 1.0 ml per litre of water.
  Approximate field quantity: 200 ml per acre in 200 L water.
- Pyraclostrobin 20% WG:
  Dosage: 1.0 g per litre of water.
  Approximate field quantity: approximately 200 g per acre in 200 L water.
- Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole:
  Dosage: 1.0 ml per litre of water.
  Approximate field quantity: approximately 200 ml per acre in 200 L water.

6. Application Precautions & Safety:
- Follow current registered product label directions and applicable CIBRC-approved guidelines.
- Use appropriate protective equipment while handling and spraying fungicides.
- Spray during calm morning or late afternoon hours ensuring uniform coverage of both leaf surfaces.

- Advise consultation with a local Agricultural Extension Officer (AEO) or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) when verification is required.
